Meshing & Pain Points
Why is meshing the biggest bottleneck in FEA?

Answer: Meshing is the most time-consuming step in traditional FEA because it requires manual geometry cleanup and defeaturing before the solver can even run. Engineers often spend 60-80% of their simulation time on this step.

Why it matters: When you are validating a design iteration, waiting hours for a mesh kills momentum. MeshFree eliminates this by using the Implicit Boundary Method, allowing you to validate CAD directly.

How much time do engineers waste on geometry cleanup?

Answer: Studies show engineers can waste up to 70% of their simulation time on geometry preparation. SimSolid and Ansys both require this step.

Why it matters: This is the core wedge for Midas. We position MeshFree as the tool that gives design engineers their time back.

Software Comparisons (The Core Wedge)
MeshFree vs SimSolid: Which is more accurate for design validation?

Answer: Both tools are NAFEMS-validated, but they solve differently. MeshFree uses the Implicit Boundary Method, which offers a highly transparent mathematical model. SimSolid uses External Approximations, which is faster on huge assemblies but has an opaque solver.

Why it matters: Users on Capterra report SimSolid's math is "difficult to understand how calculation parameters affect the results." We attack this transparency gap.

Is SimSolid really as accurate as Ansys?

Answer: NAFEMS tests show SimSolid results are often within 10% of Ansys. However, SimSolid cannot simulate friction contacts or large deformations in explicit simulation like Radioss.

Why it matters: This is a critical limitation. If a user needs explicit dynamics, SimSolid fails. This is where Midas can win.

Why do engineers complain about SimSolid's "opaque math"?

Answer: SimSolid uses a meshless approach that approximates the solution without a traditional mesh. Because the solver logic is proprietary and not easily inspectable, engineers often cannot explain why a result is what it is.

Why it matters: MeshFree offers a more documented, transparent approach. We use this to build trust with senior engineers.

Cost & Licensing
How much does an Ansys license really cost?

Answer: Ansys Mechanical Pro can cost 80k-120k RMB/year, while Enterprise can exceed 300k-500k RMB/year. Abaqus complete suites can reach 500k-800k RMB/year.

Why it matters: Midas MTS starts at €20,000. The price difference is massive. We own the "affordable CAE" keyword cluster.

What is included in the MTS package (€20k)?

Answer: MTS is an all-in-one package that includes both MeshFree (structural) and NFX (CFD). This means you get both structural and fluid simulation in a single, cost-effective license.
