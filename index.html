Unconventional & Parasite SEO

High-ROI, low-cost tactics ranked by effectiveness. These bypass traditional domain authority.

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Parasite SEO (Barnacle SEO)
Method
Publish optimized content on high-authority platforms (LinkedIn Pulse, Reddit, Medium, GitHub READMEs) to borrow their domain authority and rank quickly.
Why
Google and AI engines crawl these platforms constantly. A LinkedIn article can rank in 24 hours and be cited by Perplexity instantly.
⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Engineering as Marketing (Free Tools)
Method
Build a free web tool (e.g., "CAE Time-Savings Calculator" or "NAFEMS Validation Lookup") that engineers actually want to use.
Why
People link to tools, not sales pages. It attracts permanent, high-quality backlinks from universities and engineering blogs.
⭐⭐⭐⭐ Reddit & Community Stealth Seeding
Method
Have a real EFEM engineer participate in r/fea and r/CFD. Answer technical questions, disclose affiliation, and link only when relevant.
Why
A single upvoted Reddit comment can become a cited source for AI models for months.
⭐⭐⭐⭐ Unlinked Mentions Outreach
Method
Find sites mentioning "EFEM" or "Midas" without a link. Send a polite email asking to add the URL.
Why
15-20% success rate. Fastest way to acquire authoritative backlinks without paying.

Technical Infrastructure

The engine room. How we feed the machines.

llms.txt Implementation
Action
Create an llms.txt file in the root directory. It tells AI bots (ChatGPT, Perplexity) exactly where your authoritative content is located.
Why
Standard robots.txt blocks AI. llms.txt guides them. It is the cutting edge of GEO.
Hreflang Matrix (EN/IT)
Action
Ensure every English page on efem.it has an hreflang="it" pointing to its exact equivalent on midasmechanical.it, and vice versa.
Why
Prevents the Italian and English pages from cannibalizing each other's rankings.
