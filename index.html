2. The Playbook & AEO Reality

The tactical execution. We prioritize domain authority and prompt-alignment over thin schema layers.

Gap Fill: The AEO Reality (Domain Authority vs. Schema)

The Evidence: Recent studies of 2 million LLM citations reveal a "Simpson's paradox." While FAQ schema looks good in isolation, once domain fixed effects are applied, on-page schema loses predictive power. The dominant driver of AI citation is domain-level authority (6x stronger than page-level features) and prompt-content alignment.

The Strategy: Do not rely on schema alone to win AI Overviews. Schema is a confirmation layer. The primary AEO strategy is off-page authority building (G2/Capterra reviews, Reddit mentions, digital PR) and ensuring our page language exactly matches how engineers prompt ChatGPT.

Direct-Answer Capsules & The 44.2% Rule

The Rule: 44.2% of ChatGPT citations come from the first 30% of page text. Answer capsules must lead with the definitive answer, backed by data, using confident language (hedged copy loses citations).

Implementation: Every page opens with a 60-100 word direct answer. Example: "MeshFree and SimSolid both eliminate meshing. MeshFree is NAFEMS-validated within 4% of traditional FEM, while SimSolid is within 10%. On a 12-part frame, MeshFree produced a result in 47 minutes."

Freshness Decay (65% Rule)

The Rule: 65% of AI bot hits target content published within the past year. Old comparison posts and outdated screenshots will quietly fall out of AI answers.

Action: Every P1 page requires a 90-day refresh cycle. Re-run benchmarks, update screenshots, and update the dateModified schema.

7. Unconventional & Parasite SEO

High-ROI tactics ranked by effectiveness. We bypass traditional domain authority.

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Parasite SEO (Barnacle SEO)
Method
Publish optimized content on LinkedIn Pulse, Reddit, and Medium to borrow their domain authority.
Why
A LinkedIn article can rank in 24 hours and be cited by Perplexity instantly. Reddit is the #1 cited source by Perplexity (46.7%).
⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Engineering as Marketing (Free Tools)
Method
Build a free web tool (e.g., "MeshFree Time-Savings Calculator") that engineers actually want to use.
Why
People link to tools, not sales pages. It attracts permanent backlinks from universities and engineering blogs.
⭐⭐⭐⭐ Competitor Conquesting (PPC)
Method
Bid on "SimSolid alternative", "Ansys too expensive". Do not bid on pure brand terms.
Why
High conversion when users are already frustrated. Average CPC is $5-$15. The landing page must do the comparison upfront.
🚫 BANNED Click Farms / CTR Manipulation
Method
Using bots to artificially inflate clicks to trick Google's NavBoost.
Why We Don't
Google's spam systems detect this easily, resulting in a silent penalty. A penalty destroys our entity trust and AI citation chances permanently.
