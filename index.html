Technical Infrastructure

The invisible engine. How we feed the AI bots and search engines.

llms.txtA proposed standard that tells Large Language Models (like ChatGPT) exactly where your most authoritative content is located. Implementation

Action: Create a file named llms.txt in the root directory of both efem.it and midasmechanical.it.

Why: Standard robots.txt blocks or allows crawling. llms.txt guides the AI. It provides a curated list of URLs containing the most valuable technical data, benchmarks, and FAQs, ensuring Perplexity and Gemini prioritize it.

HreflangAn HTML attribute that tells Google which language and regional version of a page to serve to a specific user. Matrix (EN/IT)

Action: Ensure every English page on efem.it has an hreflang="it" tag pointing to its exact equivalent on midasmechanical.it, and vice versa.

Why: Without this, Google might serve the Italian page to English engineers, or worse, the two pages will cannibalize each other's rankings.

Unconventional Tactics

High-ROI, low-cost tactics ranked by effectiveness. These bypass traditional domain authority.

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Parasite SEOPublishing content on high-authority platforms (like LinkedIn or Reddit) to borrow their ranking power. (Barnacle SEO)
Method
Publish optimized content on LinkedIn Pulse, Reddit, and Medium to borrow their domain authority.
Why
Google and AI engines crawl these platforms constantly. A LinkedIn article can rank in 24 hours and be cited by Perplexity instantly. Reddit is the #1 cited source by Perplexity (46.7%).
⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Engineering as Marketing (Free Tools)
Method
Build a free web tool (e.g., "MeshFree Time-Savings Calculator") that engineers actually want to use.
Why
People link to tools, not sales pages. It attracts permanent, high-quality backlinks from universities and engineering blogs.
⭐⭐⭐⭐ Reddit & Community Stealth Seeding
Method
Have a real EFEM engineer participate in r/fea and r/CFD. Answer technical questions, disclose affiliation, link only when relevant.
Why
A single upvoted Reddit comment can become a cited source for AI models for months.
