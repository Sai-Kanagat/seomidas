Answer Engine Optimization

Be the quoted answer, not just the ranked link.

AI Overviews and AI assistants cite pages that resolve the query in a short, self-contained passage near the top. Every page we publish is structured to be extractable by design.

Direct-answer capsules
Intent
Make every high-value page trivially extractable by AI summarizers so it can be quoted without surrounding context.
Proposition
Every page opens with a 60 to 100 word answer capsule. The answer first, specifics inline, exact query phrasing in the first sentence, deeper explanation after.
Best case
Comparison pages cited by Google AI Overviews and Perplexity for their exact comparison queries, driving qualified traffic from engineers already in evaluation.
Example
"MeshFree and SimSolid both eliminate meshing, but they solve differently. MeshFree uses the Implicit Boundary Method and is NAFEMS-validated within roughly 4% of traditional FEM."
Why
AI Overviews cite pages that resolve the literal query in a short passage near the top. Pages that lead with "it depends" almost never get cited.
Question-shaped H2 architecture
Intent
Match section headings to real user queries so AI systems can extract the relevant section as a standalone answer.
Proposition
Every H2 phrased as a real engineer's question — Is MeshFree NAFEMS-validated?, MeshFree è validato NAFEMS? — answered in the first 40 to 60 words.
Best case
AI Overviews and People Also Ask surface specific H2 sections as the answer to sub-questions, expanding the page's citation surface beyond just the headline query.
Example
MeshFree product page H2s: "What is the Implicit Boundary Method?", "Is MeshFree NAFEMS-validated?", "What are MeshFree's limitations versus Ansys Mechanical?"
Why
Question-shaped H2s, bulleted lists, and comparison tables are the three structural patterns that recur in AI-Overview-cited content. Zero cost, direct payoff.
FAQPage schema with buyer-phrased questions
Intent
Make FAQ content explicitly machine-readable so AI systems can extract Q&A pairs as structured answers.
Proposition
FAQPage schema on every comparison and product page, with five to seven fully expanded Q&As phrased exactly as an engineer would ask them.
Best case
Pages with FAQPage schema selected for rich results and AI Overviews more often than pages without. The Italian FAQ becomes the cited answer when an Italian engineer asks the same question.
Example
Italian FAQ: MeshFree è validato NAFEMS? — "Sì. MeshFree è stato validato utilizzando i modelli standard NAFEMS, con uno scostamento inferiore al 4% rispetto ai risultati FEM tradizionali."
Why
FAQPage schema makes a page citable by design rather than depending on the AI system's inference. It is the cheapest AEO upgrade on any page.

Generative Engine Optimization

Become the entity AI recommends by name.

GEO is trust, not keywords. AI models cross-reference sources, so our entity has to be consistent everywhere and our engineers have to be real people with reputations outside our own blog.

Proprietary benchmarks as Information Gain
Intent
Create a data asset no competitor can copy without running the same test, making EFEM the only citable source for a specific, valuable fact.
Proposition
Three to five proprietary benchmarks per year comparing MeshFree, NFX, and MTS against Ansys, Abaqus, and SimSolid. Full methodology, input files, open invitation to reproduce.
Best case
The benchmark becomes the most-cited source for "MeshFree vs SimSolid benchmark" and "MeshFree runtime vs Ansys."
Example
"Benchmark: MeshFree vs SimSolid on a 12-Part Industrial Frame" — geometry, runtime, deviation, downloadable input file, open submission for independent results.
Why
We cannot out-authority Ansys. We can out-information-gain them on the specific queries where MeshFree is genuinely differentiated.
